## Terminal stuff

[Mac Terminal for Humans](https://www.macterminal.cc/commands) a tutorial and cheet sheet

<!--[Homebrew](https://brew.sh/) for installing things-->

See the status of all your device's network interfaces, including internal IP addresses: `if addr`
<!-- `ifconfig` -->

Find all the device (MAC) addresses on your network: `ip neigh` 🐴
<!-- `arp -a`  -->

Find your external IP address: `curl -s ipv4.icanhazip.com` or `curl -s ifconfig.me` the curl command accesses a web address from the command line

Find more details about what can be seen with your IP address: `curl -s icanhazip.com/cdn-cgi/trace`

To search within a command, follow it with `| grep ` followed by your search term and any wildcards `*` or [regular expressions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Guide/Regular_expressions/Cheatsheet) 

Bling out your terminal: [hyper](https://hyper.is/) customizable terminal/console (there are many others), like [kitty](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/)
